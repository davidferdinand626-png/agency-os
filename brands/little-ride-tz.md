# Brand Profile: Little Ride Tanzania

---

## 1. Company Overview
- **Brand Name:** Little Ride (Little Africa)
- **Market:** Dar es Salaam & Tanzania Urban Mobility
- **Headquarters:** Mwanga Tower, 9th Floor, Kijitonyama, Dar es Salaam
- **Website:** `www.little.africa`
- **Core Value Proposition:** Multimodal urban transport (Comfort sedans + nimble Bajajs), verified safety via Little Lady protocol, and automated enterprise cost-reduction (Little Corporate).

## 3. Product Offerings & Pillars
1. **Little Comfort:** Premium air-conditioned sedans with top-rated vetted drivers. Focus: comfort, meetings, reliability.
2. **Little Bajaj:** Three-wheeled nimble city runabouts. Focus: beating Dar traffic jams, speed, affordability.
3. **Little Lady:** Female drivers dedicated to female riders. 24/7 live SOS response and verified screening. Focus: peace of mind and safety.
4. **Little Corporate:** B2B corporate mobility management. One monthly consolidated invoice, zero paper receipts, saving up to 25% in travel expenses.
