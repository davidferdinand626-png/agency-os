# Customer Journey Mapping & Lifecycle Architecture

> Bridge user psychology and commercial acquisition by mapping every touchpoint, mindset shift, and friction point across the full customer lifecycle.

---

## 🎯 When to Use This Skill

Use this skill whenever you need to:
- Define and refine an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and Buyer Persona.
- Map the end-to-end path from first brand touchpoint to long-term advocacy.
- Identify drops in the conversion funnel and eliminate friction points.
- Align multi-channel content and paid acquisition strategies with buyer intent.

---

## 1. The 5 Customer Lifecycle Stages

1. **Awareness (Problem-Aware):**
   - *Customer Mindset:* "I have a problem or an unmet ambition, but I don't know who can solve it."
   - *Goal:* Stop the scroll, educate on the cost of the problem, build brand recall.
   - *Primary Channels:* Organic search, social video ads, educational content, influencer mentions.
2. **Consideration (Solution-Aware):**
   - *Customer Mindset:* "I know the solution categories available. How does your method compare to alternatives?"
   - *Goal:* Differentiate features, demonstrate unique mechanisms, establish domain authority.
   - *Primary Channels:* Comparison guides, teardowns, interactive calculators, product video demos.
3. **Decision (Product-Aware / Purchasing):**
   - *Customer Mindset:* "I want to buy, but I need to de-risk this decision for myself or my manager."
   - *Goal:* Eliminate friction, reverse risk with guarantees, provide clear pricing and ROI justification.
   - *Primary Channels:* Sales calls, case studies, free pilot/trial flows, pricing pages with transparent terms.
4. **Retention (Onboarding & Value Realization):**
   - *Customer Mindset:* "Did I make the right decision? Help me achieve my first win quickly."
   - *Goal:* Accelerate Time-to-Value (TTV), drive core product habituation.
   - *Primary Channels:* In-app onboarding tours, automated welcome email sequences, proactive account management.
5. **Advocacy (Expansion & Referral):**
   - *Customer Mindset:* "This product transformed how I operate. I want to tell my peers."
   - *Goal:* Unlock referral loops, capture video testimonials, expand contract value.
   - *Primary Channels:* Referral incentives, customer spotlight interviews, exclusive VIP community access.

## 2. Customer Journey Matrix Template

When mapping a client journey, structure the analysis using this standard matrix:

| Dimension | Stage 1: Awareness | Stage 2: Consideration | Stage 3: Decision | Stage 4: Retention | Stage 5: Advocacy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **User Goal** | Understand the root issue | Evaluate viable options | Select the best partner | Get operational value | Share results with peers |
| **Mindset & Emotion** | Frustrated, overwhelmed | Analytical, skeptical | Cautious, seeking safety | Relieved, expectant | Proud, enthusiastic |
| **Touchpoints** | LinkedIn Ad, SEO Guide | Product Page, Demo Video | Proposal, Checkout, Contract | Onboarding email, App UI | Referral link, Case study |
| **Key Questions** | "Why is this happening to us?" | "How is X different from Y?" | "What is the ROI and SLA?" | "How do I get set up today?" | "How can I help my network?" |
| **Potential Friction** | Jargon, vague headlines | Hidden pricing, long demos | Rigid contracts, complex payment | Clunky UI, slow support | No incentive or mechanism |
| **Agency Interventions** | PAS Video Ad, educational hook | Teardown article, ROI calculator | Transparent tiering, trial offer | Automated drip email series | Referral reward campaign |

## 3. Execution Protocol

1. **Step 1: Ingest Brand Context:** Review `brands/<brand-name>.md` to extract target audience, pricing, and value propositions.
2. **Step 2: Define the Primary ICP:** Document demographics, firmographics, operational KPIs, and core emotional anxieties.
3. **Step 3: Map Current vs. Desired State:** Identify where users currently drop off (e.g. high website traffic but low demo requests).
4. **Step 4: Architect Campaign Touchpoints:** Prescribe the exact content, ad creative, and email sequences needed to bridge each stage transition.
