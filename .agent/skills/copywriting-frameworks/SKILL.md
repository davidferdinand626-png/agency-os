# Copywriting Frameworks & Conversion Heuristics

> Transform marketing messaging into compelling, conversion-focused copy using proven behavioral psychology and strategic frameworks.

---

## 🎯 When to Use This Skill

Use this skill whenever you need to:
- Craft landing page headlines, subheadlines, and hero sections.
- Write high-converting ad copy for paid social (Meta, LinkedIn, Google Search, TikTok).
- Structure sales pages, pitch decks, cold emails, or product descriptions.
- Refine unclear, feature-heavy text into benefit-driven, persuasive copy.

---

## 1. Core Persuasion Frameworks

### Framework 1: PAS (Problem - Agitate - Solve)
*Best for: High-intent problem-aware audiences, pain-driven B2B and consumer products.*
1. **Problem:** State the exact, urgent pain point the customer is experiencing right now.
2. **Agitate:** Amplify the emotional and operational cost of inaction. What happens if they don't fix it today?
3. **Solve:** Present your product/service as the definitive, friction-free relief.

### Framework 2: AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action)
*Best for: Cold audiences, editorial landing pages, top-of-funnel social video ads.*
1. **Attention:** Stop the scroll with a counter-intuitive hook, bold statistic, or provocative question.
2. **Interest:** Provide a surprising insight or relatable story that maintains engagement.
3. **Desire:** Shift from features to emotional outcomes. Show the transformed "after" state.
4. **Action:** A single, unambiguous call to action (CTA) with low perceived friction.

### Framework 3: BAB (Before - After - Bridge)
*Best for: Case studies, product demo copy, SaaS landing pages.*
1. **Before:** Paint a vivid picture of the current frustrating reality.
2. **After:** Describe the dream state where the problem is completely eliminated.
3. **Bridge:** Show how your product is the easiest bridge from Before to After.

### Framework 4: The 4 U's Headline Formula
Every primary headline must satisfy at least 3 of these 4 criteria:
- **Urgent:** Why act now instead of next month?
- **Unique:** How is this fundamentally different from what competitors say?
- **Ultra-specific:** Use exact numbers, timeframes, or concrete details (not "save time", but "save 4.2 hours every Monday").
- **Useful:** What is the tangible benefit for the reader?

## 2. Value Proposition Synthesis Protocol

When formulating a core value proposition, follow Steve Blank's formula enhanced with behavioral friction reduction:

$$\text{Value Proposition} = \text{We help } [Target Audience] \text{ achieve } [Dream Outcome] \text{ without } [Top Pain Point / Friction] \text{ in } [Timeframe].$$

### Example:
- *Weak:* "Modern ride-hailing for corporate teams."
- *High-Converting:* "We help corporate finance teams in Dar es Salaam eliminate 100% of transport receipt reconciliation with automated, audited monthly invoicing."

## 3. High-Converting Call to Action (CTA) Rules

1. **First-Person Voice:** Test "Start my free trial" vs. "Start your free trial". First-person yields higher click-through.
2. **Value-Oriented Verbs:** Replace passive words ("Submit", "Click Here", "Learn More") with outcome words ("Get Instant Access", "Unlock My Custom Plan", "Book My Corporate Fleet").
3. **Friction Reducer Micro-copy:** Immediately beneath the CTA button, place a 1-line reassurance:
   - *SaaS:* "No credit card required • 2-minute setup • Cancel anytime"
   - *B2B Service:* "Guaranteed response within 15 minutes • NDA protected"

## 4. Execution Workflow

1. **Identify Customer Awareness Stage:**
   - Unaware -> Problem-Aware -> Solution-Aware -> Product-Aware -> Most Aware.
2. **Select Corresponding Framework:** Match stage to framework (Unaware = AIDA; Problem-Aware = PAS; Product-Aware = Direct Offer / Risk Reversal).
3. **Draft 3 Strategic Variants:**
   - *Variant A (Direct & Benefit-Focused):* Clear, concise, zero fluff.
   - *Variant B (Pain & Agitation-Focused):* High urgency, cost-of-inaction emphasis.
   - *Variant C (Social Proof & Authority-Focused):* Stat-heavy, testimonial-driven.
4. **Run Readability & Polish Audit:**
   - Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level: Grade 6 to 8 (accessible, punchy).
   - Cut passive voice by 90%.
   - Kill jargon ("synergy", "paradigm", "cutting-edge").
