## 1. The 4 Fundamental Email Sequence Archetypes

### Archetype 1: High-Performance B2B Cold Outbound (4 Touches)
*Goal: Secure an introductory 15-minute qualification call with a decision-maker.*

1. **Email 1: The Observation & Value Hypothesis (Day 1)**
   - *Subject Line:* [Prospect Company] transport overhead / receipt reconciliation
   - *Content:* 3-sentence observation about their team size/operations + specific pain point + peer company case proof + frictionless low-commitment question ("Worth exploring 10 mins next Tuesday?").
2. **Email 2: The Specific Case Proof (Day 4)**
   - *Subject Line:* Re: [Previous Subject]
   - *Content:* Share a 2-sentence case study: "When Company X switched their Dar es Salaam fleet to automated billing, they cut monthly transport reconciliation time from 18 hours to zero."
3. **Email 3: The Risk Reversal Pilot (Day 8)**
   - *Subject Line:* Zero-commitment fleet pilot for [Prospect Company]
   - *Content:* Offer a free 14-day trial account with dedicated concierge support and automated reporting.
4. **Email 4: The Clean Breakup (Day 14)**
   - *Subject Line:* Permission to close your file?
   - *Content:* "Assuming this isn't a priority for your team right now. I'll take a step back and won't follow up further. If transport logistics or expense reconciliation becomes an issue in Q1, feel free to reconnect."

### Archetype 2: Inbound Lead Welcome & Activation (5 Days)
*Goal: Turn a free signup or whitepaper download into an active, paying client.*
- **Day 0 (Immediate):** Delivery of promised asset + single prompt to complete the #1 core profile action.
- **Day 1:** Overcoming the biggest beginner obstacle / FAQ teardown.
- **Day 3:** Deep-dive case study with verifiable numbers and before-and-after results.
- **Day 5:** High-urgency invitation to schedule an executive onboarding session.

## 2. Subject Line Engineering Psychology

| Psychology Trigger | Formula / Pattern | High-Performing Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Observation** | `[Pain point] for [Company]` | *Corporate transport overhead for Vodacom TZ* |
| **Curiosity + Benefit** | `How [Peer Company] solved [Friction]` | *How Stanbic cut taxi receipt audits by 85%* |
| **Casual Lowercase** | `quick question regarding [Topic]` | *quick question re: corporate commutes* |
| **Stat-Driven Proof** | `[Exact Number] [Timeframe] result` | *4.2 hours saved every Friday* |

### Anti-Spam Guidelines:
- Never use all-caps words ("FREE", "URGENT", "LIMITED TIME").
- Limit exclamation points to zero.
- Maintain a minimum 60:40 text-to-HTML ratio; avoid single massive promotional images.
- Keep total outbound links per email to $\le 2$ (too many links triggers spam filtering).

## 3. Email Sequence Output Schema

```markdown
# Email Campaign Sequence: [Campaign Name]
**Target Audience:** [e.g. Chief Financial Officers / Procurement Heads]  
**Primary Goal:** [e.g. Schedule Corporate Pilot Consultation]  
**Cadence:** 4 Touches over 14 Business Days  

---

### Touch 1 (Day 1): Initial Outreach
- **Subject:** quick question re: [Company] transport audits
- **Preview Text:** Eliminating monthly receipt reconciliation
- **Body:**
  Hi [First Name],

  Noticed your team has been expanding operations across Dar es Salaam.

  Most corporate finance heads we speak with tell us that managing employee taxi receipts and petty cash reimbursements is one of their most tedious end-of-month headaches.

  We built Little Ride Corporate to replace that entire paper trail with automated monthly invoicing and verified corporate dispatch.

  Are you open to a 10-minute preview next week to see how Stanbic and Serena Hotels streamlined their fleet logistics?

  Best regards,
  [Sender Name]
```
