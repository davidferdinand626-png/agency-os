# Quality Rubric

Run this before final handoff.

## Storyboard

- The three directions are meaningfully different.
- The shot plan has a setup, turn, and payoff.
- The hero shot is obvious.
- Shot durations add up to target duration.
- Text cues do not overcrowd the frame.

## Visual Generation

- All prompts lock 2D motion graphics register.
- The visual direction is consistent across frames.
- The palette and typography do not drift.
- The subject remains recognizable.
- References are honored.

## Animation

- Motion is layer-based, not vague camera language.
- Each shot has named easing.
- Transitions are intentional.
- No shot depends on illegible generated text.
- Any provider failures are surfaced with the next best fallback.

## Remotion Overlay

- Text is readable on mobile.
- Safe-zone margins are respected.
- Animations are deterministic from frame number.
- Overlay timing matches the shot map.
- Export settings match aspect ratio and duration.

## Final Handoff

Include:

- selected direction
- frame map
- shot map
- text timeline
- provider/job IDs or manual prompt package
- consistency audit
- regeneration recommendations
