# Social Media Distribution Engine & Channel Optimization

> Convert core marketing assets into platform-native, high-engagement social posts tailored to the specific algorithms and user psychology of LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and TikTok.

---

## 🎯 When to Use This Skill

Use this skill whenever you need to:
- Repurpose long-form research, video commercials, or product announcements into social campaigns.
- Draft organic thought leadership posts, viral threads, or slide carousels.
- Write high-converting organic captions for social video releases.
- Establish a multi-platform distribution calendar for a client launch.

---

## 1. Platform-Native Formatting Blueprints

### 1. LinkedIn (B2B Authority & Carousels)
- **Primary Audience:** Corporate executives, procurement managers, HR directors, founders.
- **Hook Structure:** First 2 lines before the "see more" cutoff (under 140 characters). Must present a counter-intuitive observation or compelling business transformation metric.
- **Body Architecture:** Short, scannable paragraphs (1-2 sentences max). Generous white space between thoughts. Zero large text walls.
- **Carousel Architecture:** 6 to 10 slides saved as PDF. Each slide contains $\le 30$ words in large display typography (`28px+`).
- **Algorithm Rules:** Keep outbound links in the comments or in a post edit after 10 minutes to avoid algorithmic reach penalties. Use 2-3 focused industry hashtags max.

### 2. X / Twitter (High Velocity & Provocative Insights)
- **Primary Audience:** Tech founders, investors, operators, early adopters.
- **Thread Anatomy:**
  - *Tweet 1 (The Hook):* A bold claim + quantifiable proof + the promise of a teardown.
  - *Tweets 2-7 (The Core Value):* One distinct, actionable takeaway per tweet, accompanied by an image, code snippet, or diagram.
  - *Final Tweet (The CTA & Summary):* A concise synthesis with an explicit call to retweet Tweet 1 and visit the project repository.
- **Length Constraint:** Max 280 characters per tweet; punchy, sharp, zero fluff.

### 3. TikTok & Instagram Reels (Audio/Visual Hooks)
- **Primary Audience:** Consumers, mobile-first audiences, culture trendsetters.
- **The 3-Second Hook Rule:** First 3 seconds must combine:
  1. High-contrast visual movement or kinetic text reveal.
  2. Spoken auditory hook stating an urgent pain point or curiosity trigger.
- **Caption Structure:** Max 150 characters above the fold with a clear question to spark comments ("Which fleet feature would save your team the most time?").

## 2. Omnichannel Content Repurposing Workflow

When an agency campaign asset (e.g. video commercial or case study) is produced, run this 4-step distribution cascade:

```
[Core Campaign Asset: e.g. 35s Video Commercial + Research Report]
                       │
       ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
       ▼               ▼               ▼
[LinkedIn Post]   [X/Twitter Thread] [Short-Form Reels/TikTok]
Corporate Case    Data-heavy         Kinetic hook
Study + Founder   industry           targeting end-user
Perspective       teardown           convenience & speed
```
