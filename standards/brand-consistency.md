# Standard: Brand Consistency & Design Tokens

---

## 1. Brand Tokens Architecture
Every brand handled by Agency OS must maintain a single source of truth in `brands/<brand-name>.md`. Tokens include:
- Primary Color (`brand-primary`)
- Secondary Accent (`brand-secondary`)
- Deep Background (`brand-background-dark`)
- High-Contrast Text (`brand-text-light`)
- Semantic Tones (Safety, Alert, Success)

## 2. Enforcement Rules
1. **No Rogue Hex Codes:** Never inject unapproved neon or pastel hues that conflict with the client palette.
2. **Official Logo Preservation:** Logos must never be stretched, rotated abnormally, or altered in aspect ratio. Safe margins around the logo must equal at least 50% of the logo's height.
3. **Tone of Voice:**
   - **Consumer Mobility (e.g. Little Ride):** Energetic, empowering, accessible, reassuring, bilingual (Swahili & English).
   - **Enterprise Advisory (e.g. Stratiq):** Confident, analytical, restrained, modern, data-driven.
