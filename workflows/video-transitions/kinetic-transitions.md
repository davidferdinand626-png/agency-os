# Workflow: Kinetic Video Transitions & Timing
**Track:** Visual Rhythm & Continuity

---

## Objective
Standardize and execute seamless transitions between scenes in 2D/3D motion graphics.

## Standard Transition Catalog

| Transition Type | Visual Technique | Audio Cue | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Whip Pan / Shutter Zoom** | Rapid directional camera blur + scale kick ($1.0 \to 1.15 \to 1.0$) | High-frequency whoosh | Fast scene changes between high-energy acts |
| **Light Wipe / Laser Slice** | Angled linear gradient streak across canvas | Laser hiss / electric sweep | Transitioning from dark problem space into bright solution |
| **Glass Card Morph** | UI container expands or slides into center frame | UI click / subtle pop | Introducing product features or fleet choices |
| **Forcefield Shield Wipe** | Expanding hexagonal / circular outline from center | Resonant synth swell / shield hum | Safety, security, or protection feature reveals |
| **Kinetic Type Push** | Next scene's bold typography pushes current plate off screen | Sub-bass drop / impact | High-tempo social cuts & promo codes |
| **Grand Dissolve** | Smooth opacity fade to deep brand background ($0.8\text{s}$) | Shimmering bell decay | Final closing lockup & legal footer |

## Implementation Rules
1. Never exceed $0.4\text{s}$ for mid-video transitions (prevents visual drag).
2. Transition effects must start $0.15\text{s}$ before scene end and resolve $0.15\text{s}$ after next scene begins.
3. Every visual transition must have an accompanying sound design cue.
