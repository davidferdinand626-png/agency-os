# Workflow: Commercial Video Production
**Track:** Cinematic Commercial Advertising

---

## Objective
Direct, assemble, sound-design, and render a broadcast-grade 30–40 second commercial using local engines (Chrome Headless Canvas or CapCut).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Dramatic Arc Construction:**
   - Use the `emotional-arc-designer` skill:
     - Act 1: Friction & Problem (Hook the viewer in first 3 seconds).
     - Act 2: Introduction of Hero Brand / App.
     - Act 3: Fleet / Feature Differentiation (Side-by-side comparison).
     - Act 4: Trust, Safety & Reassurance Pillar.
     - Act 5: Enterprise / Cost-Efficiency Proof.
     - Act 6: Grand Finale, Promo Voucher & Clear CTA.
2. **Audio-First Sound Architecture:**
   - Select or synthesize high-energy musical bed (e.g. 122 BPM Afro-tech).
   - Place timed Foley and SFX at scene transition points (whooshes, app pings, engine rumbles, cash chimes).
   - Place brand sonic mnemonic at the closing lockup.
3. **Multi-Track Timeline Assembly:**
   - Track 0: Background video/plate footage.
   - Track 1: Dynamic overlay badges, glass cards, and product cutouts.
   - Track 2: Kinetic typography and promotional voucher.
   - Track 3: Master audio stream.
4. **Rendering & Export Pipeline:**
   - Execute automated render using Chrome Headless with `MediaRecorder` or generate native CapCut draft for timeline editing.
   - Output high-bitrate MP4 file with verified codecs.
