# Workflow: Market Research & Intelligence
**Track:** Research & Competitive Strategy

---

## Objective
Generate a complete, verifiable market analysis and competitive intelligence report for a target brand, sector, or geography.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Intake & Scope:**
   - Define target product category, geographical boundary (e.g. Dar es Salaam, East Africa), and customer demographic.
2. **Quantitative TAM / SAM / SOM Modeling:**
   - Calculate Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) using the `market-sizing-analysis` skill.
   - Ground baseline parameters in verifiable demographic data (population, smartphone penetration, commuting density).
3. **Competitive Matrix:**
   - Execute the `competitor-teardown` skill against the top 3 incumbent competitors.
   - Compare pricing models, fleet availability, driver commission rates, mobile money integration, and trust factors.
4. **Customer Pain Point Extraction:**
   - Identify top 3 friction points in incumbent solutions (e.g., surge pricing, unvetted drivers, traffic delays).
5. **Report Synthesis:**
   - Output structured analysis with executive summary, quantitative tables, differentiation roadmap, and strategic recommendations.
