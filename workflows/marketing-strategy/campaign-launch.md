# Workflow: Multi-Channel Campaign Launch
**Track:** Marketing Strategy & Growth

---

## Objective
Design, budget, and schedule a complete go-to-market campaign across paid, organic, influencer, and PR channels.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Campaign Core Angle & Offer:**
   - Define hero value proposition (e.g., "Smart moves in Dar traffic").
   - Establish high-converting introductory promo code (e.g., `KARIBUDAR` - 20% off first 3 rides).
2. **Channel Allocation Blueprint:**
   - Use the `launch-strategy` skill to structure a 30-day / 90-day phase-gated roadmap.
   - Allocate budget across Paid Digital (Meta, Google, TikTok), Local OOH, Influencer Marketing, and Activation.
3. **Influencer & Creator Roster:**
   - Use the `influencer-marketing` skill to segment creators:
     - Tier 1: Culture & Lifestyle Key Opinion Leaders (macro reach).
     - Tier 2: Commuter & Daily Urban Hustle creators (hyper-local trust).
     - Tier 3: Tech & Business Professionals (B2B Little Corporate advocates).
   - Generate creative brief and compliance guidelines for each tier.
4. **B2B Pipeline (If Applicable):**
   - Outline enterprise outreach strategy for corporate travel accounts.
5. **Measurement & KPI Framework:**
   - Define Day 1, Day 30, and Day 90 targets for downloads, ride activations, CAC, and retention.
