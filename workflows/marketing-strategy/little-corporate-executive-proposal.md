## 1. Executive Summary: The Cost of Paper Taxi Receipts

In Dar es Salaam, enterprise finance teams lose an average of **18 to 22 hours per month** managing manual transportation reimbursements. The reliance on paper taxi receipts, unmetered street cabs, and petty cash advances creates four chronic operational liabilities:

1. **Expense Inflation & Receipt Fraud:** Studies across regional corporate fleets show an estimated **15% to 25% budget leakage** from inflated manual receipts and duplicate reimbursement claims.
2. **Administrative Friction:** Finance and audit teams spend days cross-referencing trip times with paper receipts and manual logbooks.
3. **Safety & Duty-of-Care Liabilities:** Unvetted street taxis lack real-time GPS tracking, identity verification, and 24/7 emergency dispatch.
4. **TRA Tax Compliance Headaches:** Paper receipts frequently fail Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) electronic fiscal compliance audits, complicating VAT deductions.

## 2. The Solution: Little Corporate Platform

**Little Corporate** replaces the entire paper trail with an automated digital fleet management ecosystem:

```
[Employee Hails via App] ───► [Billed to Corporate Account] ───► [Consolidated Monthly VAT Invoice]
Zero Out-of-Pocket Cash       GPS Route & Cost-Center Tagged     100% TRA & Audit Compliant
```

### Core Platform Capabilities
- **Zero Paper, Zero Petty Cash:** Employees travel using their authorized corporate profile. No personal money is spent; no expense claims are filed.
- **Custom Policy Controls:** Administrators define spend parameters:
  - Allowed hours (e.g. 06:00 to 20:00 for daytime staff; 24/7 for shift personnel).
  - Geographical geofencing (e.g. trips restricted to Greater Dar es Salaam or designated office hubs).
  - Mandatory project codes or cost centers tagged prior to ride dispatch.
- **Monthly Consolidated Billing:** One single, itemized, TRA-compliant VAT invoice delivered on the 1st of every month with itemized GPS route logs, employee IDs, and department summaries.
- **ERP Integration:** Direct automated export to SAP, Oracle ERP, QuickBooks, and Microsoft Dynamics via secure REST API or automated Excel/CSV pipelines.
