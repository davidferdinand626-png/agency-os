# Workflow: Storyboard to Motion Graphics
**Track:** Motion Design & Animation

---

## Objective
Convert a marketing concept and reference assets into a structured storyboard, shot list, kinetic typography timeline, and executable motion graphics composition.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Intake & Asset Ingestion:**
   - Review user brief, duration target (e.g. 15s, 30s, 35s), aspect ratio (9:16 or 16:9), and client assets.
2. **Three Strategic Storyboard Directions:**
   - Execute the `motion-graphics-director` skill:
     - Direction A: Fast-Paced Kinetic / Urban Hustle (Punchy cuts, high typography).
     - Direction B: Sleek Premium / Product Elegance (Fluid transitions, cinematic depth).
     - Direction C: Story-Driven Character / Problem-Solution (Relatable commuter journey).
3. **Shot Mapping & Scene Breakdown:**
   - Break selected direction into discrete acts with microsecond precision:
     - `Scene ID`, `Duration (us)`, `Visual Description`, `On-Screen Text`, `Audio FX Cue`.
4. **Programmatic Composition Setup:**
   - Structure timeline in Remotion (React) or HTML5 Canvas/WebGL composition.
   - Apply brand design tokens for colors, type scales, and gradient overlays.
5. **Kinetic Typography Integration:**
   - Overlay synchronized typography with staggered reveals, spring physics, and accent pills.
